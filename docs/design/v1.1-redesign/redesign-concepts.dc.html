Turn 1 · four directions

CareerVault, four ways

Each concept keeps everything the app does today — log by chat, import a résumé, browse entries, generate a tailored résumé, set your check-in cadence — and changes the shape it arrives in. 1a is a written record. 1b is a momentum dashboard. 1c is a permanent archive. 1d is a companion you talk to. Items marked NEW don't exist in the code yet.

1a

The Ledger

Quiet, personal, light. Your career as something you write.

Editorial and unhurried — a bound journal rather than an app. Entries live on one continuous dated column, so scrolling is literally reading your own history back. Momentum is present but whispered: a thin year spine in the margin, not a scoreboard.
